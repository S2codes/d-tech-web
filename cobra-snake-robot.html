MISSION-PROVEN ARCHITECTURE

Where conventional
robots struggle.

Bridging the mobility gap in disaster zones, narrow conduits, and hazardous ruins.

Unlike traditional wheeled or tracked rovers that easily get trapped in rubble or narrow conduits, COBRA uses continuous distributed articulation. Its elongated body snakes through narrow openings, climbs 25° slopes, traverses rough rubble, and brings multi-spectral sensing directly to the critical point of interest.

A proprietary integration of bio-inspired mechanics, multi-spectral sensing payloads, and intelligent edge computing designed for extreme survival scenarios.

01 / BIO-INSPIRED LOCOMOTION

26 Degrees of Freedom &
multi-gait agility.

Powered by 20 kg/cm serial bus servo motors across 15 modular segments, COBRA adapts its gait in real time. It executes inchworm crawling, sidewinding, and tight rotations with a 250 mm turning radius and zero ground clearance—traversing gravel, wet concrete, soil, and metallic surfaces.

Search, rescue & survivor detection
