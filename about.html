ABOUT THE COMPANY

Engineering indigenous
deep-tech solutions.

INCUBATED AT I-HUB, IIT DELHI

DTECH is an Indian deep-tech technology enterprise focused on developing indigenous hardware and software solutions in advanced robotics, autonomous systems, artificial intelligence, intelligent sensing and specialised engineering.

Solving challenges in hazardous
and high-risk environments.

Incubated at I-Hub, IIT Delhi, DTECH operates at the intersection of mechanical engineering, electronics, embedded computing, robotics and software intelligence to solve issues of hazardous and high risk environments. The company develops integrated technological solutions combining intelligent hardware, autonomous capabilities, sensor systems and real-time data processing for complex and high-risk environments.

DTECH aims to contribute to India's indigenous technology development ecosystem by developing next-generation robotic platforms and engineering solutions for space exploration, remote inspection, defence, disaster management, infrastructure monitoring and other specialised applications.
